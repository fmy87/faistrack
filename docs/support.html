Common Questions

Why isn't a drive being detected automatically?
FaisTrack needs to be open in the foreground to detect and record a drive automatically. Open the app before you start driving.

How do I delete my account?
Go to More → Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your profile, drives, cars, and tracks you created.

How do I remove a friend?
Swipe left on a friend in the Friends tab, or open their profile for more options.

How do subscriptions work?
Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through your Apple ID. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription any time in iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.
